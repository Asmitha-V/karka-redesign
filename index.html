Questions people ask first

Still unsure? Talk to the team.

What is Pay After Placement?

You learn without upfront costs and share your success, along with the course fee, only after you land a tech job with an agreed minimum salary.

Is placement guaranteed?

No one can strictly guarantee a job. Because Karka is paid after you are, the academy succeeds only when you do, and that is why it works hard to make you job ready.

Do I need a coding or CSE background?

No. The curriculum takes you from curious beginner to confident professional, whether you studied arts or science or you're switching careers.

How long does a course take?

It depends on the program, but most run 6 to 8 months, including intensive training, project work and internship time.

Will I get internship or project experience?

Yes. A large part of the curriculum is live projects and simulations that mimic a real corporate environment.

Who are the mentors?

Industry veterans from tech companies, who keep the curriculum aligned with what employers expect today.
